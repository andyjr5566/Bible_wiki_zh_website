創世記 6:14-16 · Genesis 6:14-16
14 你要用歌斐木造一隻方舟，分一間一間地造，內外抹上松香。
Make yourself an ark of gopher wood. Make rooms in the ark, and cover it inside and out with pitch.
15 方舟的造法乃是這樣：要長三百肘，寬五十肘，高三十肘。
This is how you are to make it: the length of the ark 300 cubits, its breadth 50 cubits, and its height 30 cubits.
16 方舟上邊要留透光處，高一肘。方舟的門要開在旁邊。方舟要分上、中、下三層。
Make a roof for the ark, and finish it to a cubit above. Set the door of the ark in its side. Make it with lower, second, and third decks.
